Codex image model & quality

The bundled Codex CLI requests GPT Image 2 and sets quality and native size automatically. This route uses your subscription allowance. Choose a paid GPT Image 2.5 model for explicit API quality controls.

Size and quality by model

GPT Image 2.5: Low through Max quality, or Auto. The 4K selection is capped to a non-experimental canvas: at most 2560 pixels on either edge and 3.69 megapixels total. The composer shows the actual requested dimensions. Auto quality shows a price range.

Nano Banana: native 1K, 2K, or 4K. Nano Banana 2 offers Minimal or High thinking; Pro manages thinking automatically. Thinking is billed separately from image output. Both use automatic image quality.

Paid API images retain native pixels. Google ratios such as 16:10 are cropped from the nearest supported canvas; the original is saved. Codex 2K/4K exports use a 2048/4096-pixel long edge and can involve a local resize.
